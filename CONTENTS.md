## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document is the canonical navigation index for the **Strategic Structural Intelligence and Competitive UIRTU-Relay (SSICR)** research repository. SSICR extends structural intelligence and UIRTU-Relay from primarily cooperative composition to competitive, mixed-motive, and institutionally mediated interaction among strategic subjects. It investigates how large-scale AI-supported strategy generation and simulation can produce contextual comparative-advantage knowledge, revisable strategic worldviews, and open-ended structural improvement **without transferring strategic sovereignty away from the subjects entitled to exercise it**.

The repository contains **10 research manuscripts, 4 proposed technical specifications, 3 illustrative cases, and 8 planned figure assets**, alongside navigational and release materials. These are research proposals and conceptual artifacts, not claims of empirically validated system performance.

## 4. Technical Specifications (`specs/`)

The four specifications describe proposed interoperability contracts. They are intended to be implemented, challenged, and revised through conformance testing rather than treated as established standards.

| Specification | Scope | Principal artifacts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SPEC-001 — Strategic Subject and Relay Contract](specs/SPEC-001-Strategic-Subject-and-Relay-Contract.md) | Subject identity, permissions, Relay contracts, boundaries, and revocation | Subject and Relay contract records |
| [SPEC-002 — Strategy and Simulation Schema](specs/SPEC-002-Strategy-and-Simulation-Schema.md) | Strategies, scenarios, experiments, cross-play, and scoring | Strategy, scenario, and run records |
| [SPEC-003 — Strategic Advantage Map Schema](specs/SPEC-003-Strategic-Advantage-Map-Schema.md) | Conditional advantage claims, comparators, evidence, uncertainty, and failure regions | Strategic Advantage Map (SAM) |
| [SPEC-004 — Worldview and Counter-Evidence Schema](specs/SPEC-004-Worldview-and-Counter-Evidence-Schema.md) | Principles, worldview trees, revision, counter-evidence, and authorization | Worldview forest and evidence records |

**Dependency direction:** subject/Relay contracts → strategy and simulation records → advantage-map claims → principles and worldview revisions. Evidence, uncertainty, governance, and OTHERS considerations operate across all stages.

## 8. Evaluation and Falsifiability

Research claims should be tested against explicit baselines and alternative explanations. The proposed validation program emphasizes:

- **Reproducibility:** recorded configurations, seeds, versions, and provenance.
- **Comparative validity:** fixed baselines, adaptive opponents, and cross-play.
- **Generalization:** held-out environments, institutions, opponents, and time horizons.
- **Calibration:** uncertainty, coverage gaps, and abstention where evidence is insufficient.
- **Counter-evidence:** persistent negative findings and explicit failure boundaries.
- **Governance:** authorization, reversibility, auditability, and human control.
- **Pluralism:** no assumption that distinct legitimate goals collapse into one scalar utility.

See [TESTABLE-HYPOTHESES.md](TESTABLE-HYPOTHESES.md) and [VALIDATION-ROADMAP.md](VALIDATION-ROADMAP.md) for proposed experimental detail once those files are available.
