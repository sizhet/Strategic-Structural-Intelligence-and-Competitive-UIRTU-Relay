Strategic Structural Intelligence and Competitive UIRTU-Relay (SSICR) is a research framework for studying how autonomous strategic subjects, intelligent units, and Relay structures interact, compete, cooperate, and co-evolve. It proposes large-scale multi-strategy simulation, context-dependent Strategic Advantage Maps, evidence-grounded Strategic Worldview Forests, and open-ended structural recursive self-improvement, while preserving human strategic sovereignty through explicit authorization, counter-evidence, and auditable decision boundaries. The v1.0.0 research repository includes 10 conceptual papers, 4 technical specifications, 3 illustrative case studies, and a validation agenda with testable hypotheses. The framework and examples are research proposals, not claims of completed empirical validation. Authors: Sizhe Tan & GPT-Obot. GitHub repository: https://github.com/sizhet/Strategic-Structural-Intelligence-and-Competitive-UIRTU-Relay . DOI: pending Zenodo release.
